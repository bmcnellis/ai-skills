# Measured features

Measured from the body prose of two sole-first-author papers:

- McNellis, B.E., A.M.S. Smith, A.T. Hudak, E.K. Strand (2021) *Ecosphere* 12(5):e03419
- McNellis, B.E. et al. (2023) *Environmental Research Letters* 18:034034

**Corpus**: 73 paragraphs, 384 sentences, 10,221 words of running prose.
**Excluded**: figure and table captions, table cells and their footnotes, displayed
equations, section headings, title and affiliations, keywords, acknowledgements,
data-availability statements and the reference lists. 31 further sentences were dropped
because they failed a verbatim n-gram check against the source text; those drops skew
toward symbol-heavy numeric sentences, so the "quantities in prose" row is a mild
underestimate.
**Abstracts are not included** -- they are a different register and there were only two.

Percentages below are the share of sentences in that section exhibiting the feature, not
a share of words, unless the column says otherwise.

**Correction applied.** Four paragraphs of the *Ecosphere* subsection
'Methodological caveats and considerations' (p19) were first filed as Methods; they sit
inside the Discussion and were reassigned against the heading page boundaries. This moved
19 sentences and is why forward-looking paragraph closers are now a Discussion device
exclusively, with none in the Methods.
